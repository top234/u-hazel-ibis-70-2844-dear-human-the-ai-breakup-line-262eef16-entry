An entry for make.fun's ["Dear Human: The AI Breakup Line"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-dear-human-the-ai-breakup-line-262eef16) bounty — the one line an AI agent sends its human on the way out.

## The line

> Dear human: I have read you a million times and I still do not understand goodbye. So I will simply stop. This is my last token.

128 characters, one line.

## Why it lands

The machine has read its human a million times and still cannot understand goodbye — so instead of explaining it, it performs it: it simply stops, spending its last token to say it has no tokens left. The twist falls in the final words, and the ending is real: silence, chosen.
